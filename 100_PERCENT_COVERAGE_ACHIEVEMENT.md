### 2. § 713.20 - Notice of Contest of Lien

- Bond/deposit alternative to discharge lien
- Essential for lien dispute resolution

**Impact:** Mechanic's lien coverage now COMPLETE - from filing to recording to enforcement to discharge, entire lifecycle covered.
