## ✅ Statutes Added (7 Total)

### Session 1: Landlord-Tenant (Ch. 83) - 2 Statutes

1. **§ 83.53 - Landlord's Access to Dwelling Unit**
   - 12-hour notice requirement for non-emergency entry
   - Protects tenant privacy and quiet enjoyment
   - Complements existing habitability and eviction statutes
   - **Impact:** Completes landlord-tenant entry/access framework

2. **§ 83.64 - Retaliatory Conduct** ⭐ CRITICAL
   - Prohibits landlord retaliation against tenants
   - 6-month presumption of retaliation
   - Key tenant protection statute
   - **Impact:** Essential for eviction defenses

**Ch. 83 Total:** 11 statutes (most comprehensive chapter)

---

### Session 2: Personal Injury/Damages (Ch. 768) - 3 Statutes

3. **§ 768.81 - Comparative Fault** ⭐ CRITICAL
   - Modified comparative fault (50% bar rule)
   - Foundation for ALL personal injury cases
   - Applies to negligence and strict liability
   - **Impact:** Enables proper analysis of PI cases

4. **§ 768.73 - Punitive Damages**
   - Standards for punitive damages in Florida
   - Clear and convincing evidence standard
   - Caps at 3x compensatory (or $500K minimum)
   - **Impact:** Complete damages framework

5. **§ 768.76 - Evidence of Profits**
   - Admissibility of defendant's wealth/profits
   - Complements punitive damages statute
   - Financial condition evidence
   - **Impact:** Practical guidance for punitive claims

**Ch. 768 Total:** 3 statutes (NEW chapter coverage)

---

### Session 3: Insurance (Ch. 627) - 2 Statutes

6. **§ 627.702 - Valued Policy Law** ⭐ CRITICAL
   - Total loss = full policy limits (unique to Florida)
   - Applies to property insurance
   - Key for hurricane/fire total losses
   - **Impact:** Fundamental Florida insurance principle

7. **§ 627.428 - Attorney's Fees (Insurance)**
   - Mandatory fee-shifting in insurance litigation
   - Applies to bad faith, breach, and policy disputes
   - Levels playing field against insurers
   - **Impact:** Critical fee recovery statute

**Ch. 627 Total:** 6 statutes (comprehensive insurance coverage)
