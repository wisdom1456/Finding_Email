# Quick Guide: Two Letter Versions

## 🎯 The Point

Your system generates **TWO versions** of every letter:

| Version | For | Contains Citations? | Purpose |
|---------|-----|---------------------|---------|
| **📧 Client Letter** | Client | ❌ No | Send to client - clean & professional |
| **📚 Attorney Letter** | Attorney | ✅ Yes | Internal review - verify facts |

---

## 📍 Where to Find Them

After running analysis, look for these tabs:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📧 Client Letter (Clean) │ 📚 Attorney Letter │ ...     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🔍 Example: Same Sentence, Two Versions

### 📧 Client Letter (Clean)
```
On November 14, 2024, you entered into a contract with 
LLW Construction, Inc. for $128,000. You paid $100,000 
to date, but the contractor ceased work in March 2025.
```

### 📚 Attorney Letter (With Citations)
```
On November 14, 2024 (Source: Contract.pdf), you entered 
into a contract with LLW Construction, Inc. (Source: Contract.pdf) 
for $128,000 (Source: Contract.pdf). You paid $100,000 to date 
(Source: Payment_Records.pdf), but the contractor ceased work 
in March 2025 (Source: Client_Notes.txt).
```

---

## ✅ Recommended Workflow

1. **Review Attorney Letter first** → Verify facts have sources
2. **Review Client Letter second** → Check readability
3. **Send Client Letter** → Download PDF/EML and send
4. **Keep Attorney Letter** → Save to case file

---

### Attorney Letter

## 💡 Pro Tips

✨ **For Client Calls:** Review Attorney Letter before client meetings to have sources ready for questions

✨ **For Court Filings:** Use Attorney Letter to quickly cite source documents in motions

✨ **For New Associates:** Give them Attorney Letter to learn how to properly cite sources

✨ **For Quality Control:** Quick scan of Attorney Letter shows if any facts lack sources

---

## 🚨 Red Flags to Check

When reviewing Attorney Letter, watch for:

❌ Dates without citations  
❌ Dollar amounts without citations  
❌ Claims without sources  
❌ "[Source verification needed]" placeholders  

If you see these, the analysis may need additional documents.
