Legal Issues and Analysis

  • Nonpayment of Rent / Breach of Lease
    • The tenant’s failure to pay full rent for April and May 2025 and nonpayment for June 2025 constitutes a material breach.
    • Under § 83.56(3), you may serve a 3-day notice to pay the full past-due rent or surrender possession. The 3 days exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays.
    • Acceptance of partial payment after serving the 3-day notice can waive or require re-issuance of the notice unless handled per statute; avoid accepting partial payments without a compliant written stipulation. See § 83.56(5)(a).
  • Recovery of Monetary Damages
    • Rent and Late Fees: Recoverable if authorized by the lease. Late fees must be contractually specified and reasonable (not punitive or unconscionable).
    • Administrative Fee ($1,000): Enforceability depends on clear lease authorization and characterization as liquidated damages or agreed administrative charges. If the lease expressly provides for such fees upon default/collections, courts generally enforce if not a penalty.
    • Attorney’s Fees/Costs: Available to prevailing party per § 83.48 and any lease fee-shifting clause.
    • Interest: Pre-judgment interest is available on liquidated sums; post-judgment interest per Fla. Stat. § 55.03.
  • Possession / Keys / Holdover
    • Failure to return keys and vacate impedes surrender of possession. Do not change locks or self-evict; proceed via court process (§ 83.67).
    • If tenant remains in possession after June 30, 2025, you may claim damages for holdover; double rent may be available for willful holdover (§ 83.58), subject to evidentiary showing and judicial discretion.
    • Upon lawful retaking of possession, comply with security-deposit notice obligations within 30 days (§ 83.49(3)).
  • Procedural Posture and Venue
    • File the eviction for possession and damages in Duval County Court if the tenant fails to cure. Include Count I (Possession) and Count II (Damages). Serve summons and complaint per Florida Rules of Civil Procedure and ch. 83.

Risks and Considerations

  • Lease Language Controls Fees: The administrative fee and specific late fees must be expressly authorized by the lease to be recoverable.
  • Partial Payment Handling: Acceptance of any partial rent after service of the 3-day notice can require a new notice or a compliant stipulation; mishandling can delay eviction (§ 83.56(5)(a)).
  • Collections Conduct: Communications to the tenant should avoid any conduct prohibited by Fla. Stat. § 559.72 (FCCPA).
  • Active Duty Status: Verify whether the tenant is a servicemember; if so, additional protections under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act may apply.

Recommendations and Next Steps (High Priority)
