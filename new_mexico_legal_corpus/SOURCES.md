# New Mexico Legal Corpus - Sources Reference

This document provides official source links and citation guidance for New Mexico legal research.

## Official Sources

### 1. New Mexico Compilation Commission (NMOneSource)
The official source for NMSA 1978 and NMRA (Rules).
- **URL**: [https://nmonesource.com/nmos/nmsa/en/nav.do](https://nmonesource.com/nmos/nmsa/en/nav.do)
- **Use Case**: Primary source for verbatim text and official annotations.

### 2. New Mexico Legislature
- **URL**: [https://www.nmlegis.gov/Legislation/BillLookup](https://www.nmlegis.gov/Legislation/BillLookup)
- **Use Case**: Checking current bill status and effective dates for new legislation.

### 3. New Mexico Supreme Court
- **URL**: [https://supremecourt.nmcourts.gov/rules/](https://supremecourt.nmcourts.gov/rules/)
- **Use Case**: Accessing the latest approved rules and committee commentary.

### 4. Authorized Secondary Repositories (Justia / FindLaw)
- **Justia**: [https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/](https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/)
- **FindLaw**: [https://codes.findlaw.com/nm/](https://codes.findlaw.com/nm/)
- **Use Case**: User-friendly display of statutes and historical annotations.

## Citation Formats

### Statutes (NMSA 1978)
New Mexico statutes should be cited as follows:
- **Formal**: NMSA 1978, § 57-12-2 (2019)
- **Alternative**: N.M. Stat. Ann. § 57-12-2
- **Common**: Section 57-12-2 NMSA 1978

### Rules (NMRA)
New Mexico Rules Annotated should be cited as follows:
- **Format**: Rule 1-004 NMRA
- **Format (Magistrate)**: Rule 2-401 NMRA
- **Format (Metropolitan)**: Rule 3-401 NMRA

## Attorney Verification Guide

To verify a citation or research further:
1. **Locate the ID**: Every entry in the `statutes.jsonl` or `nm_rules.jsonl` has a `source_urls` field.
2. **Visit the Link**: Click the provided link to go directly to the official source (NMOneSource or Justia).
3. **Check Versioning**: Ensure the `source_doc_version` matches the current year unless specifically referencing historical law.
4. **Compare Verbatim**: Our corpus uses `text` fields for verbatim matches. In case of discrepancy, the official NMOneSource text is the final authority.
