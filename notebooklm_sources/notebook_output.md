### Slide 2: The Problem: Inefficient Intake
*   **Time Drain:** Manual document review requires hours of reading to extract basic facts, dates, and parties.
*   **Low-Value Work:** This initial review does not require senior attorney judgment but consumes valuable time.
*   **Consistency Risks:** Creating findings letters from scratch is time-consuming and leads to structural inconsistencies.
*   **Citation Errors:** Manual citation across multiple documents is error-prone and creates liability risk.

**Speaker Notes:**
We are solving for the bottleneck in case intake. The goal is to remove the "busywork" of reading 50 pages just to understand the basic timeline, allowing you to focus on strategy immediately.

***

### Slide 3: The Solution: Harvey AI
*   **Production Software:** A live system that analyzes legal documents and generates findings letters.
*   **Automated Extraction:** Extracts text, identifies key facts, and constructs timelines from PDF, Word, and image files.
*   **Hallucination Prevention:** Validates all statute citations against a verified legal corpus for Florida and New Mexico.
*   **Verifiable Output:** Every fact in the generated letter is cited directly to its source document.

**Speaker Notes:**
Think of this tool as a tireless junior associate. It handles the initial review and drafting, giving you an 80% complete draft. You remain responsible for the final review and approval,.

***

### Slide 4: The Attorney Workflow
*   **Step 1: Create Case:** Initialize a case record with client name and reference number.
*   **Step 2: Upload Documents:** Drag-and-drop files or import directly from Clio,.
*   **Step 3: Run Analysis:** AI identifies legal issues and processes documents (1–8 minutes),.
*   **Step 4: Review Results:** Verify the generated findings letter, timeline, and document summaries.
*   **Step 5: Export:** Edit the letter in-browser and export to HTML/Word for final polish.

**Speaker Notes:**
The workflow is designed to be simple. There is no complex manual to study. You can run your first analysis in less than 5 minutes.

***

### Slide 5: Supported Documents & Intake
*   **Broad Format Support:** Accepts PDF (digital/scanned), DOCX, TXT, CSV, JPG, PNG, EML, and HTML.
*   **Integrated OCR:** Automatically processes scanned documents and images using GPT-4o Vision.
*   **Clio Integration:** Connects via OAuth to import matters, documents, and notes directly.
*   **Quality Management:** "Verification Hub" allows you to fix or exclude poor-quality documents before analysis.

**Speaker Notes:**
For best results, upload core documents first: intake forms, police reports, contracts, and key correspondence. The system handles files up to 50MB each.

***

### Slide 6: Findings Letters & Citations
*   **Professional Format:** Generates structured, attorney-style letters (Introduction, Findings, Analysis, Conclusion).
*   **Source Linking:** Every fact includes an inline citation (e.g., `[Client_Intake.pdf]`).
*   **Instant Verification:** Click any citation to view the original source document and verify the fact in context.
*   **Draft Status:** All outputs are drafts designed for attorney review, not final work product.

**Speaker Notes:**
This is the most critical feature for trust. You do not have to guess where a fact came from. You can audit the AI's work by clicking the citations.

***

### Slide 7: Quality, Accuracy & Control
*   **Verified Legal Corpus:** AI checks citations against 51 verified Florida statutes and 42 New Mexico statutes.
*   **Hallucination Guardrails:** Citations are rejected if they do not match the verified corpus, preventing invented laws.
*   **Jurisdiction Limits:** Optimized *only* for Florida and New Mexico civil litigation.
*   **Attorney Responsibility:** You must verify that cited statutes are strategically applicable to the specific case.

**Speaker Notes:**
The system prevents the AI from inventing laws. However, it cannot replace your judgment on whether a law is *appropriate* for the case strategy. You are the final decision-maker.
