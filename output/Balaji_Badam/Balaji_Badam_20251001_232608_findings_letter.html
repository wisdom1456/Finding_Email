Legal Analysis (Florida Law — Summary)

Under Florida landlord-tenant and contract law, landlords may pursue contractual remedies for unpaid rent and enforce late fees where those fees are reasonable and provided for in the lease. Remedies typically include a civil action for unpaid rent and related charges, and initiation of summary eviction procedures where appropriate. Before filing, landlords commonly issue a written demand for payment and, if nonpayment continues, may proceed with forcible entry and detainer (eviction) or a separate monetary claim in county court. Pursuit of an administrative or time-expense fee is generally governed by the lease terms and must be supported by the contract language and documentation of incurred expenses.
