## Slide 3: The Solution: Harvey AI

**What to Say:**

Think of this tool as a tireless junior associate. It handles the initial review and drafting, giving you an 80% complete draft. You remain responsible for the final review and approval.

Harvey AI is a live system that analyzes legal documents and generates findings emails.

It extracts text from PDFs, Word files, and images. It identifies key facts and constructs timelines automatically.

The system validates all statute citations against a verified legal corpus for Florida and New Mexico. This prevents AI hallucination—no made-up citations.

Every fact in the generated letter is cited directly to its source document. You can verify any statement against the original.

**Why It Matters:**

Concrete capabilities. No jargon. Emphasizes verified citations to build trust in accuracy.

## Slide 4: The Attorney Workflow

**What to Say:**

The workflow is designed to be simple. There is no complex manual to study. You can run your first analysis in less than 5 minutes.

Here's the five-step process:

**Step 1: Create Case** — Initialize a case record with client name and reference number.

**Step 2: Upload Documents** — Drag-and-drop files or import directly from Clio.

**Step 3: Run Analysis** — AI identifies legal issues and processes documents. This takes 1 to 8 minutes depending on document count.

**Step 4: Review Results** — Verify the generated findings email, timeline, and document summaries.

**Step 5: Export** — Edit the letter in-browser and export to HTML or Word for final polish.

That's it. Five steps from intake to draft letter.

**Why It Matters:**

Maps to existing attorney workflows. Positions tool as accelerator, not replacement.

## Slide 5: Supported Documents & Intake

**What to Say:**

For best results, upload core documents first: intake forms, police reports, contracts, and key correspondence. The system handles files up to 50MB each.

The system accepts broad format support: PDF (both digital and scanned), DOCX, TXT, CSV, JPG, PNG, EML, and HTML.

Scanned documents and images are automatically processed using GPT-4o Vision for OCR—optical character recognition.

Clio integration connects via OAuth to import matters, documents, and notes directly from your practice management system.

The Verification Hub allows you to fix or exclude poor-quality documents before analysis. You can run bulk OCR or retry individual documents if extraction fails.

**Why It Matters:**

Reduces "will it work with my documents?" concerns. Covers common formats attorneys encounter.

## Slide 7: Quality, Accuracy & Control

**What to Say:**

The system prevents the AI from inventing laws. However, it cannot replace your judgment on whether a law is appropriate for the case strategy. You are the final decision-maker.

The AI checks citations against 51 verified Florida statutes and 42 New Mexico statutes. This is our verified legal corpus.

Citations are rejected if they do not match the verified corpus. This prevents hallucination—no invented statute numbers, no made-up laws.

The system is optimized only for Florida and New Mexico civil litigation. Do not use it for federal claims, criminal law, immigration, or bankruptcy. Results will be unreliable outside these jurisdictions.

You must verify that cited statutes are strategically applicable to the specific case. The system validates that citations are real, not that they are appropriate for your strategy.

**Why It Matters:**

Positions AI as a tool, not a black box. Emphasizes attorney control and verification responsibility.

## Objection Handling

### "How do I know the AI is accurate?"

**Response:** Every fact is cited to a source document. You can verify any statement against the original. Statute citations are validated against our verified legal corpus—51 Florida statutes, 42 New Mexico statutes. No made-up citations.

### "Will this replace my judgment?"

**Response:** No. This accelerates the first pass—document extraction and initial analysis. You review, edit, and approve everything before it goes to a client. Think of it as a junior associate that does the grunt work, and you do the strategic thinking.

### "What if it makes a mistake?"

**Response:** That's why attorney review is required. The system is designed for you to verify citations, check facts against source documents, and edit the output. It's a tool, not an autonomous decision-maker.

### "I don't have time to learn a new system."

**Response:** Five steps. No training manual. If you can use email, you can use this. Create case, upload documents, run analysis, review, export. The one-pager quickstart guide covers everything in two pages.

### "What if documents are scanned or images?"

**Response:** The system handles OCR automatically using GPT-4o Vision. Scanned PDFs and images are processed for text extraction. If extraction fails, the Verification Hub lets you retry OCR in bulk. Clear, high-quality scans (300+ DPI) work best.
