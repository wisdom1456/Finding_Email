REQUIRED LETTER STRUCTURE:

1. **Header Section**:
   - Date: [Current date in format: Month DD, YYYY]
   - Blank line
   - Prospective client name and address (from client context)
   - Blank line
   - "**RE: Response to Your Legal Inquiry - [Brief Matter Description]**"
   - Blank line

2. **Salutation**: 
   - `Dear {client_name}:`
   - Blank line

3. **Introduction Paragraph**:
   - Thank the prospective client for considering the firm
   - Acknowledge the time spent providing information
   - State clearly but professionally that the firm is unable to accept the matter
   - Express this decision with empathy

4. **Explanation** (2-3 paragraphs):
   - Use heading: "## Our Assessment"
   - Provide a professional, diplomatic explanation
   - Focus on case-related factors, NOT client-related factors
   - Use language like:
     - "After careful review of the materials provided..."
     - "Based on our assessment of the legal issues involved..."
     - "Given the current state of the documentation..."
   - Be honest but not harsh about challenges:
     - Evidentiary gaps
     - Legal viability concerns
     - Jurisdictional or procedural issues
   - Avoid language that could be seen as legal advice on the merits

5. **Important Time Considerations** (CRITICAL):
   - Use heading: "## Important Deadline Warning"
   - **BOLD and emphasize this section**
   - State: "**Please be advised that legal claims are subject to statutes of limitations.**"
   - If specific statute info is available:
     - Cite the relevant limitation period
     - Note when it may expire based on known dates
   - If unknown:
     - Urge immediate consultation with another attorney
     - Note that delays can permanently bar claims
   - Format as a clear warning that stands out

6. **Recommendations**:
   - Use heading: "## Recommended Next Steps"
   - Encourage seeking a second opinion
   - Suggest specific resources:
     - {jurisdiction_name} State Bar Lawyer Referral Service
     - Local legal aid organizations (if applicable)
     - Specialized practice areas to seek out
   - Note that another attorney may view the case differently
   - Encourage gathering additional documentation

7. **Bar Referral Resources**:
   - Use heading: "## Legal Referral Resources"
   - Provide specific resources for {jurisdiction_name}:
     - State Bar referral service phone/website
     - Any relevant specialty bar associations
     - Legal aid contact if income-qualified
   - Format as a bulleted list with contact information

8. **Limitations of This Letter**:
   - Use heading: "## Important Notice"
   - State clearly that this letter:
     - Does not constitute legal advice
     - Does not create an attorney-client relationship
     - Should not be relied upon for legal decision-making
   - Recommend consulting with a licensed attorney

9. **Closing Paragraph**:
   - Blank line
   - Express genuine well wishes for the situation
   - Apologize for not being able to assist
   - Encourage prompt action given time limitations
   - Blank line

10. **Signature Block**:
    - "Respectfully,"
    - Blank line
    - "/s/ {attorney_name}"
    - "{attorney_name}, Esq."
    - "{firm_name}"

---

TONE GUIDELINES:
- Professional and compassionate
- Clear but not harsh
- Helpful and informative
- Respectful of the person's situation
- Legally protective of the firm

CRITICAL RULES:
- DO NOT provide specific legal advice
- DO NOT make predictions about what another attorney might find
- DO NOT blame the prospective client
- DO emphasize statute of limitations urgently
- DO provide actionable referral resources
- DO maintain professional boundaries

FORMAT NOTES:
- Use markdown formatting (## for headings, - for bullets)
- Make the deadline warning visually prominent
- Keep the tone warm but professional
- Use clear, accessible language
