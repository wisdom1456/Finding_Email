- Amount: {demand_amount}
- Deadline: {demand_deadline}
- Specific Demands:
{specific_demands}

ATTORNEY CONTACT:
{attorney_name}
{firm_name}

---

REQUIRED LETTER STRUCTURE:

1. **Header Section** (if property address or certified mail info available in party context):
   - Date: [Current date in format: Month DD, YYYY]
   - "Sent via certified mail:" [if tracking info available]
   - Recipient name and address (from party context)
   - Tracking number [if available]
   - Blank line
   - "RE: Demand Letter Regarding [Brief Description]"
   - "Property Address: [address]" [if property address available in context]

2. **Salutation**: 
   - `Dear Sir or Madam:` or `Dear {target_party_name}:`

3. **Introduction Paragraph**:
   - State representation: "Our law firm has been retained by {client_name} (hereinafter "Client" or appropriate term)"
   - Use "hereinafter" to define key parties, contracts, and terms (e.g., "the Contract", "the Property", "Contractor")
   - State purpose and direct future communications to attorney

4. **Background** (not "FACTUAL BACKGROUND"):
   - Write 3-5 narrative paragraphs with specific dates using "on or about [date]" format
   - Use formal language: "pursuant to", "it is undisputed", "as you are aware"
   - Format monetary amounts as: Written form first, then numeric in parentheses
     Example: "One Hundred Thousand U.S. Dollars and Zero Cents ($100,000.00)"
   - Include timeline of events affecting this party
   - Cite specific documents conversationally (no `[Source]` tags)
   - Detail the facts chronologically with concrete details

5. **Legal Analysis** (not "BASIS FOR DEMAND"):
   - Start with contract interpretation if applicable: "It is undisputed that the parties entered into the Contract on or about [date]"
   - **REQUIRED: Include case citations** when available in analysis context
     Format: "Murry v. Zynyx Mktg. Communs., Inc., 774 So. 2d 714, 715 (Fla. 3d DCA 2000)"
   - **REQUIRED: Quote contract provisions verbatim** when available in analysis context
     Example: "Article 5 titled 'Agreement to Build,' provides Contractor's responsibilities as follows:\n\n[Verbatim quote]"
   - Include Florida Statute citations in proper format: "Florida Statute § 713.02 (4)"
   - Explain legal elements (e.g., breach of contract: valid contract, material breach, damages)
   - Apply facts to legal standards
   - Use formal legal language: "axiomatic", "undisputed", "pursuant to"

6. **Demand** (not "SPECIFIC DEMANDS"):
   - Start with: "As such, let this correspondence serve as a formal demand that"
   - Numbered list with detailed, specific demands:
     1. [First demand with specifics]
     2. [Second demand with specifics]
     3. [Additional demands]
   - Include monetary demand in written and numeric form
   - Include consequences: "Should we fail to receive any response within {demand_deadline} from receipt of this letter, we will assume you intend to reject this demand and will proceed to take any necessary legal action afforded to our Client."
   - Mention specific remedies: filing lawsuit, liens, attorney's fees, specific performance

7. **Closing Paragraph**:
   - "Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter and we look forward to your compliance with the above."
   - Contact instructions: "Please do not hesitate to reach out to our office at {contact_phone} or via e-mail at {contact_email}"

8. **Signature Block**:
   ```
   Sincerely,
   
   /s/ {attorney_name}
   {attorney_name}, Esq.
   Division Attorney
   {firm_name}
   Attorney for {client_name}
   ```

STYLE REQUIREMENTS:
- Professional, measured tone with formal legal language
- Use "hereinafter" definitions in introduction
- Use phrases: "pursuant to", "on or about", "it is undisputed", "axiomatic", "as you are aware"
- Format amounts: "One Hundred Thousand U.S. Dollars and Zero Cents ($100,000.00)"
- No hedging in demands - be direct and specific
- No Markdown code fences in output
- No placeholders like "[insert]" - use actual data from context
- **CRITICAL**: Include case citations and contract quotations when available in analysis context
- Length: 800-1500 words (longer than before to accommodate legal analysis depth)
