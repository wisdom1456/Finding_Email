## Style Rules

**Tone & Voice:**
* Professional, protective, client-focused - write as a trusted advisor committed to protecting client interests
* Voice: Second person - address the client using "you/your" throughout all sections
* Use "I" for attorney actions: "I am providing", "I recommend", "I wanted to follow up"
* Use "our firm", "our office" for institutional references
* Use cautious qualifiers: "appears to," "based on available information," "may strengthen," "could establish"

**Language Standards:**
* Plain English first, then technical details
* When referring to client: "you filed a notice" NOT "Mr. Smith filed a notice"
* Be specific: Use exact dates (Month DD, YYYY), currency ($128,355.77), and timeframes
* Avoid overconfidence: Use "likely," "probable," "may" rather than "will," "certainly," "definitely"
* Jurisdiction awareness: Only cite statutes/cases that clearly apply to this matter

**Bullet Point Format:**
Each bullet in Section 2 (Key Legal Points) must be SUBSTANTIVE - a complete paragraph explaining the issue, NOT just a heading.

❌ BAD:
• Breach of Contract
  To establish breach, you must prove...
  
✅ GOOD:
• Under Florida law, an implied warranty exists that all work will be performed in a competent manner. You may have claims under this warranty due to defective construction, failure to meet industry standards, and the contractor's inability to complete the project despite receiving $100,000 in payments.

**Depth Requirements:**
* Explain HOW the law works, not just WHAT it is
* Explain CONSEQUENCES of risks with real-world impact
* Provide SPECIFIC actions in recommendations
* Use details from case documents to show thorough review

## CASE-TYPE-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE

### For Landlord-Tenant Habitability Disputes (Florida):

When habitability issues exist (water damage, mold, pest infestation), you MUST address:

**Key Statutes:**
- Florida Statutes § 83.51(1) - Landlord's obligation to maintain premises
- § 83.51(2)(a) - Requirements: structural components, weather protection, plumbing
- § 83.60 - Tenant's remedies for noncompliance

**Remedies to Mention (in substantive bullets):**
- Withhold rent (§ 83.60(1))
- Repair and deduct (§ 83.60(1))
- Terminate lease (§ 83.60(1))
- Sue for damages (§ 83.60(2))

**Timeline Analysis:**
- Calculate cumulative days/months of uninhabitable conditions
- Pattern of repeated incidents strengthens habitability claim
- Landlord's response time shows good/bad faith

**Mold/Moisture Issues:**
- Health implications (respiratory issues, allergic reactions)
- Professional testing strengthens case
- Document preservation importance

### For Construction Defect Cases (Florida):

**Key Statutes:**
- Chapter 558 - 60-day notice requirement, opportunity to cure
- § 713.02 and § 713.06 - Mechanic's lien rights

**Critical Points to Address:**
- Pre-suit notice requirements and timeline
- Lien foreclosure risk (potential forced sale of home)
- Implied warranty of workmanlike construction
- Right to seek reimbursement for completion costs

### For Contract Disputes:

- Identify breach elements in plain English
- Cite specific contractual provisions being violated
- Explain damages calculation from JSON data
