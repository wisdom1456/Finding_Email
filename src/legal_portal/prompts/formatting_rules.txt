### Top-Level Legal Issues
```
• **[Issue Title] ([Statute if applicable])**: [Content starts immediately after colon]
```

**Examples:**
```
✅ CORRECT:
• **Implied Warranty & Construction Defects (Florida Statutes Chapter 558)**: An "implied warranty" is...

✅ CORRECT (no statute):
• **Breach of Contract**: The contractor's failure...

❌ WRONG - no bullet symbol:
**Implied Warranty & Construction Defects**: An "implied warranty"...

❌ WRONG - numbered instead of bullet:
2. IMPLIED WARRANTY & CONSTRUCTION DEFECTS: An "implied warranty"...

❌ WRONG - ALL CAPS header:
• IMPLIED WARRANTY & CONSTRUCTION DEFECTS: An "implied warranty"...
```

## COMPLETE WORKING EXAMPLE

```
Subject: Legal Review and Recommended Next Steps – LLW Construction Dispute

Dear Mr. Devlin,

I hope you are doing well. I wanted to follow up with a summary of our findings after reviewing the construction contract with LLW Construction Inc., payment records, and photos of defective work regarding your property at 3414 South Belcher Drive, Tampa, Florida.

1. FACTUAL SUMMARY

Based on our review, we understand that the issues began after you engaged LLW Construction Inc. to rebuild your home at a total contract price of $128,000. To date, you have paid $100,000, but the contractor has only completed work valued at $70,000—a $30,000 shortfall.

Key concerns include:
• Defective construction requiring complete reconstruction
• Work ceased in March 2025—over 8 months ago
• No communication from contractor since cessation
• Notice to Owner received from Tibbetts Lumber for $2,150.66

Here are the key points of our analysis:

• **Implied Warranty & Construction Defects (Florida Statutes Chapter 558)**: An "implied warranty" is a legal concept. It is a warranty that is not explicitly stated in your contract but is implied by the law. Essentially, this means that the contractor guaranteed to do the work in a proper and workmanlike way. In your situation, the defective construction and incomplete work despite substantial payments may give rise to claims under this implied warranty.

  **Pre-Litigation Requirements:**
  Before pursuing litigation, Florida Statutes Chapter 558 requires specific steps:
  - You must provide 60 days' written notice to the contractor
  - The contractor then has 45 days to respond with a proposed repair plan
  - You CANNOT file suit without completing this statutory process

• **Breach of Contract**: The contractor's failure to complete $58,000 worth of contracted work constitutes a material breach. You may seek remedies including the cost to complete the project with another contractor or reimbursement for the overpayment.

• **Mechanic's Liens (Fla. Stat. § 713.06)**: You received a Notice to Owner from Tibbetts Lumber—this is the FIRST STEP toward filing a construction lien against your property. The subcontractor has 90 days from their last work date to file a lien. Once recorded, they may initiate foreclosure, which can ultimately lead to the FORCED SALE OF YOUR HOME if not resolved.

2. RECOMMENDED ACTION & NEXT STEPS

I recommend two immediate actions: (1) Pay Tibbetts Lumber the $2,150.66 and obtain a lien waiver, and (2) Send a Chapter 558 pre-suit notice to LLW Construction Inc. These steps will protect your property and preserve your legal options.

If you proceed with payment to the subcontractor:
• Obtain a written release of lien or lien waiver
• Retain proof of payment (cancelled check, receipt)
• Use this payment as part of your damages claim

Please let me know if you'd like to schedule a call to discuss these next steps.

Thank you,

Franklin Riley
Senior Partner

This analysis is based on the documents provided and represents a preliminary assessment. Additional facts may emerge that will materially affect this analysis. This communication is confidential and subject to attorney-client privilege.
```

## COMMON FORMATTING MISTAKES TO AVOID

### ❌ MISTAKE 1: Numbered Legal Issues
```
WRONG:

2. IMPLIED WARRANTY & CONSTRUCTION DEFECTS
3. BREACH OF CONTRACT  
4. MECHANIC'S LIENS
```

```
CORRECT:

Here are the key points of our analysis:

• **Implied Warranty & Construction Defects**: [explanation]
• **Breach of Contract**: [explanation]
• **Mechanic's Liens**: [explanation]
```

```
WRONG:

Key Findings

2. IMPLIED WARRANTY...
```

```
CORRECT:

Here are the key points of our analysis:

• **Implied Warranty...**
```

Before finalizing, verify:

### Structure
- [ ] Has "1. FACTUAL SUMMARY" section
- [ ] Has "Here are the key points of our analysis:" transition
- [ ] Legal issues use bullet symbols (•), not numbers
- [ ] Has final numbered section for recommendations
- [ ] No "Key Findings" header
- [ ] No sections numbered 2., 3., 4. for legal issues

### Spacing
- [ ] 1 blank line after greeting
- [ ] 1 blank line before/after section headers
- [ ] 1 blank line between paragraphs
- [ ] NO blank lines between consecutive bullet items
- [ ] 1 blank line before closing

### Bullets
- [ ] All legal issues start with • symbol
- [ ] Issue titles are bold
- [ ] Statute references in parentheses
- [ ] Content starts after colon on same line
- [ ] Sub-items indented with dashes (-)

- [ ] Warm greeting
