## FLORIDA-SPECIFIC LEGAL GUIDANCE

### Construction Defect Procedures (Chapter 558)
In Florida, the "Notice and Opportunity to Repair" process is governed by Chapter 558, Florida Statutes.
- **60-Day Notice**: The claimant must serve a written notice of claim on the contractor at least 60 days before filing a lawsuit.
- **45-Day Response**: The contractor has 45 days to respond, which may include an offer to repair, a monetary settlement, or a denial of the claim.
- **Mandatory Process**: This is a condition precedent to filing a lawsuit.

### Mechanic's Liens (Chapter 713)
- **Notice to Owner (NTO)**: Subcontractors must serve an NTO within 45 days of starting work.
- **Claim of Lien**: Must be recorded within 90 days of final furnishing of labor/materials.
- **Foreclosure**: A lien remains valid for 1 year; a lawsuit to enforce must be filed within that year.

### Landlord-Tenant (Chapter 83)
- **Security Deposits**: Landlord has 15 days to return the deposit or 30 days to give notice of intention to impose a claim.
- **Habitability**: Governed by § 83.51, requires structural integrity, plumbing, and weather protection.
