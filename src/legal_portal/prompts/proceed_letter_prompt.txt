REQUIRED LETTER STRUCTURE:

1. **Header Section**:
   - Date: [Current date in format: Month DD, YYYY]
   - Blank line
   - Client name and address (from client context)
   - Blank line
   - "**RE: Engagement Confirmation - [Brief Case Description]**"
   - Blank line

2. **Salutation**: 
   - `Dear {client_name}:`
   - Blank line

3. **Introduction Paragraph**:
   - Thank the client for choosing the firm
   - Confirm the firm's decision to proceed with representation
   - Express confidence in the case based on the analysis

4. **Case Assessment Summary** (2-3 paragraphs):
   - Use heading: "## Case Assessment"
   - Summarize key strengths of the case
   - Note the overall documentation completeness
   - Highlight the primary legal claims to be pursued
   - Mention any minor gaps that will be addressed during representation

5. **Next Steps** (numbered list):
   - Use heading: "## Next Steps"
   - Opening: "To move forward efficiently, we will proceed with the following:"
   - Numbered list of 4-6 specific action items:
     1. Prepare and send demand letter to opposing party
     2. Continue gathering any supplemental documentation
     3. Begin detailed case timeline construction
     4. Research applicable case law and precedents
     5. Establish communication schedule with client
   - Include estimated timeline for demand letter (e.g., "within 5-7 business days")

6. **Client Responsibilities**:
   - Use heading: "## What We Need From You"
   - Brief paragraph about client's role
   - List any documents or information still needed
   - Request prompt notification of any new developments

7. **Fee Arrangement Reference**:
   - Brief paragraph referencing the fee agreement
   - Note: "[Fee arrangement details to be confirmed separately]"

8. **Closing Paragraph**:
   - Blank line
   - Reaffirm commitment to the client's interests
   - Encourage questions and open communication
   - Provide direct contact information
   - Blank line

9. **Signature Block**:
   - "Sincerely,"
   - Blank line
   - "/s/ {attorney_name}"
   - "{attorney_name}, Esq."
   - "{firm_name}"
   - "Attorney for {client_name}"

---

TONE GUIDELINES:
- Professional and confident
- Reassuring but not overpromising
- Clear and actionable
- Build client trust and rapport

FORMAT NOTES:
- Use markdown formatting (## for headings, - for bullets, numbered lists)
- Keep paragraphs focused and readable
- Avoid legal jargon where plain language works
