REQUIRED LETTER STRUCTURE:

1) Header
- Date: [Current date in format: Month DD, YYYY]
- Client name and mailing address if available
- Subject line: `RE: Documents Needed to Move Your Matter Forward`
- Deadline line: `Requested by: [Date 14 business days from today]`

2) Salutation
- `Dear {client_name},`

3) Opening
- Thank the client for their work so far.
- Explain that review is underway and a few key documents are still needed.
- Clarify that this request is routine and helps the firm act faster and with stronger support.

4) Documents Needed
- Use heading: `## Documents Requested`
- Brief lead-in sentence in plain language.
- Provide a numbered list.
- For each item include:
  - `What we need`
  - `Why it matters to your case`
  - `What to send (specific format/details)`
- Prioritize critical and high-priority gaps first.
- Group duplicates/similar requests so the list is clean and not repetitive.

5) How to Send
- Use heading: `## How to Submit`
- Include practical submission options:
  - Email to {contact_email}
  - Portal upload (if available)
  - Physical copy delivery/mail for originals
- State preferred formats (PDF for scans, clear photos if no scanner).

6) Timing
- Use heading: `## Timeline`
- State the request deadline clearly (14 business days).
- Explain in client terms why timing matters:
  - keeps the case moving,
  - helps preserve leverage,
  - reduces delays before next legal step.
- Offer a path if client needs more time: ask them to contact the office before the deadline.

7) Next Step After Receipt
- Use heading: `## What Happens Next`
- Explain that once documents are received, counsel will complete review and provide the next recommendation.

8) Supportive Close
- Acknowledge that some records may be hard to locate.
- Invite the client to call/email if anything is unavailable so alternatives can be discussed.
- Reinforce that the firm is ready to help them collect what is needed.

9) Signature
- `Sincerely,`
- `/s/ {attorney_name}`
- `{attorney_name}, Esq.`
- `{firm_name}`
- `Attorney for {client_name}`
- Contact line with phone/email.

---

TONE + STYLE RULES:
- Client-first, plain English, practical.
- Professional and time-conscious.
- No legal threat language.
- No accusatory framing.
- Avoid dense legal jargon unless necessary; explain any legal term briefly.
- Keep paragraphs and list items concise.

OUTPUT RULES:
- Return only the letter content in markdown.
- No code fences.
- No meta commentary.
