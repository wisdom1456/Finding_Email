REQUIRED LETTER STRUCTURE:

1. **Header Section**:
   - Date: [Current date in format: Month DD, YYYY]
   - Blank line
   - Client name and address (from client context)
   - Blank line
   - "**RE: Document Request - [Brief Case Description]**"
   - "**Response Required By: [Date 14 business days from today]**"
   - Blank line

2. **Salutation**: 
   - `Dear {client_name}:`
   - Blank line

3. **Introduction Paragraph**:
   - Thank the client for engaging the firm
   - Explain that after initial review, additional documentation is needed
   - Emphasize the importance of these documents to case success
   - Note that gathering these materials is a normal part of the process

4. **Documents Needed** (detailed list):
   - Use heading: "## Documents Requested"
   - Opening: "To build the strongest possible case, we need the following:"
   - Blank line
   - For EACH missing document or gap, create a numbered entry with:
     - **Document Name/Type**: Clear description
     - **Why It Matters**: 1-2 sentences on legal importance
     - **What to Provide**: Specific format or details needed
   - Example format:
     ```
     1. **Contract or Agreement Documents**
        - *Why It Matters*: The original contract establishes the terms that may have been breached and defines the parties' obligations.
        - *What to Provide*: Original signed contract, any amendments, and related correspondence about contract terms.
     ```
   - Group similar documents together
   - Prioritize critical gaps first, then high-priority

5. **How to Provide Documents**:
   - Use heading: "## How to Submit"
   - List multiple options:
     - Email: {contact_email} (preferred for electronic copies)
     - Mail: [Firm address]
     - Client Portal: [If applicable]
   - Note acceptable formats (PDF preferred, original documents for key items)
   - Mention that the firm can arrange pickup for physical documents if needed

6. **Timeline and Importance**:
   - Use heading: "## Timeline"
   - Emphasize the 14 business day deadline
   - Explain why timing matters:
     - Statute of limitations considerations
     - Building case momentum
     - Preparing demand letter or legal action
   - Offer to discuss extensions if circumstances require

7. **What Happens Next**:
   - Brief paragraph on next steps once documents received
   - Note that the firm will review and may request clarification
   - Mention that this moves the case toward action (demand letter, filing, etc.)

8. **Questions or Difficulties**:
   - Acknowledge client may have trouble locating some items
   - Encourage reaching out if any document is unavailable
   - Offer to discuss alternatives or workarounds

9. **Closing Paragraph**:
   - Blank line
   - Reiterate importance of prompt response
   - Express commitment to the client's case
   - Provide direct contact information
   - Blank line

10. **Signature Block**:
    - "Sincerely,"
    - Blank line
    - "/s/ {attorney_name}"
    - "{attorney_name}, Esq."
    - "{firm_name}"
    - "Attorney for {client_name}"

---

TONE GUIDELINES:
- Professional but approachable
- Urgent but not alarming
- Helpful and solution-oriented
- Clear about what's needed and why

FORMAT NOTES:
- Use markdown formatting (## for headings, - for bullets, numbered lists)
- Make document list scannable with bold headings
- Keep explanations concise but informative
- Use formatting to highlight the deadline
