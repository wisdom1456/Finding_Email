LEGAL ISSUES

BREACH OF CONTRACT

A breach of contract occurs when one party fails to fulfill their obligations under the agreement. In your case, the failure of LLW Construction to complete the work as agreed and to meet the quality standards specified in the contract constitutes a potential breach. You may be entitled to seek damages for the incomplete and substandard work, including the cost of hiring a replacement contractor and any additional expenses incurred. [Source: Construction Contract]

NEGLIGENCE

Negligence involves a failure to exercise reasonable care, resulting in damage or harm. The substandard workmanship and delays you have experienced may support a claim of negligence against LLW Construction. This claim would require demonstrating that the contractor failed to perform the work with the skill and care expected in the industry, leading to your financial losses. [Source: Emails and Visual Evidence]

UNJUST ENRICHMENT

Unjust enrichment occurs when one party benefits at the expense of another in an unfair manner. Given that you have paid $100,000 without receiving the promised services, you may have a claim for unjust enrichment. This would involve seeking restitution for the payments made for services not rendered or completed as agreed. [Source: Payment Records and Construction Contract]
