LEGAL ISSUES

BREACH OF WARRANTY OF HABITABILITY

Under Florida law, landlords are required to maintain rental properties in a condition fit for human habitation. This includes addressing issues such as water intrusion and mold. The management’s failure to adequately address the water intrusion and refusal to test for mold may constitute a breach of this warranty. You may be entitled to remedies such as lease termination or rent reduction.

NEGLIGENCE IN PROPERTY MAINTENANCE

Negligence in property maintenance occurs when a landlord fails to take reasonable care to maintain the property, leading to damage or injury. The ongoing water intrusion and the management’s inadequate response could support a claim of negligence. This claim may allow you to seek damages for any harm caused by the management’s failure to act.

UNFAIR TRADE PRACTICES

Unfair trade practices involve deceptive or unfair business practices. The offer of a rental concession in exchange for a release that limits your rights may be considered an unfair trade practice. If proven, this could entitle you to additional remedies under Florida law.
