LEGAL ISSUES

BREACH OF CONTRACT

Legal Standard: Under Florida law, a breach of contract occurs when one party fails to fulfill their obligations under the contract. The elements include the existence of a valid contract, a material breach, and damages resulting from the breach.

Application to Facts: The contract with LLW Construction is valid, and their failure to complete the work as agreed constitutes a potential material breach. The payments you have made without receiving the contracted services support this claim.

Defenses/Risks: LLW Construction may argue that any delays or quality issues were due to unforeseen circumstances or that they are still within a reasonable timeframe to complete the work. Procedural hurdles may include proving the extent of the breach and the resulting damages.

Remedies/Damages: You may be entitled to monetary damages, including reimbursement for payments made, costs to hire a replacement contractor, and any additional repair costs incurred.

Procedural Requirements: It is important to document all communications and attempts to resolve the issues with LLW Construction, as this will be crucial in any legal proceedings.

NEGLIGENCE

Legal Standard: Negligence in construction involves failing to exercise the care that a reasonably prudent contractor would under similar circumstances, resulting in damage or loss.

Application to Facts: The substandard work and failure to complete the project may indicate negligence on the part of LLW Construction. This could be supported by evidence of industry standards and expert evaluations of the work performed.

Defenses/Risks: LLW Construction may contend that the work met industry standards or that any deficiencies were corrected. Establishing negligence requires demonstrating that the contractor’s actions fell below the standard of care.

Remedies/Damages: You may be entitled to damages for the cost of repairs and any additional expenses incurred due to the contractor’s negligence.

Procedural Requirements: Gathering expert evaluations and documenting all defects will be essential in pursuing a negligence claim.
