Key Provisions

Misrepresentation & Failure to Disclose:

  • Under Florida law, sellers are required to disclose known facts that materially affect the value of the property and are not readily observable.
  • Misrepresentation occurs when false statements are made that induce a party to enter into a contract.
  • These provisions may apply to your case if it is determined that the seller knowingly provided false information or failed to disclose material facts.

Florida Statutes - General Contract Law:

  • Contracts must be performed in good faith, and parties are obligated to adhere to the terms agreed upon.
  • If a party fails to meet these obligations, they may be liable for breach of contract, which could include remedies such as rescission or damages.
