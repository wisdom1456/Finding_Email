Key Provisions

Misrepresentation and Failure to Disclose (Florida Statutes Chapter 475 and Chapter 689):

  • Florida law requires sellers to disclose all known facts materially affecting the value of the property that are not readily observable.
  • Misrepresentation occurs when a seller provides false or misleading information that influences a buyer’s decision.
  • The seller’s failure to disclose the property’s flood history could form the basis of a legal claim for damages [Source: 142PropertyDisclosure_3b2a8c85.pdf].

Flood Damage and Insurance Claims:
